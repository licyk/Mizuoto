**Voices** is the voice library. A voice can be used again and again in **Speak**, **Projects** and **Tools**; when you change models, Mizuoto works out how the new model can use it.

There are four kinds:

| Kind | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **From a clip** | One or more reference clips with their transcripts, for cloning |
| **Designed** | A voice a design model made from a description; the clip it spoke becomes its main clip, so other models can clone it too |
| **Preset** | A model's preset speaker, for that model only |
| **Trained** | A voice with its own fine-tuned weights, from training (see [Train](/docs/guide-train)) |

Each voice is a folder under `voices/<name>/` in the data folder (`voice.json` and FLAC clips in `references/`). The list is a scan of those folders, so a voice folder copied in appears too.

## The list

At the top: **Search voices**, chips to filter by kind, **Import a voice** and **New voice**. Each card shows the name, kind, description, language, **Clips: N** and their total length; **Play its clip** plays the main clip, and **Use in Speak** opens Speak with the voice chosen. Click a card to open the voice's page.

## A new voice

Click **New voice**, type a **Name**, then choose a way:

<Tabs items={["From audio", "Design", "Preset"]}>
<Tab value="From audio">

1. Add one or more reference clips: drop or choose files, **Record** (3 to 30 seconds of clear speech works best), **Server files** or **Earlier results**.
2. Each clip gets a **What is said in the clip** field. It may stay empty: **Missing transcripts are filled by speech recognition when it is installed.**
3. Choose the **Language** (or **Any language**).
4. Click **Create**.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Design">

You need an installed model that designs voices, such as Qwen3-TTS 1.7B VoiceDesign; without one the dialog says **No design model is installed.**

1. Choose the **Design model**.
2. Describe the voice under **The voice, described**, for example "A warm, low male voice, calm and a little hoarse".
3. **What the voice says in its sample** is the sentence to listen to; change it if you like.
4. Click **Generate**. Each click makes one candidate under **Candidates** (the last 4 are kept); listen to each.
5. Click **Keep this one** beside the one you like: it becomes a **Designed** voice.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Preset">

1. Choose the **Model** (only installed models with preset speakers are listed).
2. Choose the **Speaker**.
3. Click **Create**.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Any result can become a voice too: **Keep as a voice** in its menu.

## The voice's page

The top shows the name and kind; a designed voice also shows **Designed with** *model* and its description, a preset voice **Speaker** *name* **of** *model*.

### Reference clips

(Not for preset voices.) One row per clip: a play button, the waveform, its length and sample rate, and:

- the **Main clip** badge, or **Make it the main clip**. The main clip comes first when the voice is cloned, and is the one played from the list.
- a remove button (one clip must remain).
- below it, the clip's transcript; click **Save** (or press Enter) after changing it.

**Transcribe again** has speech recognition transcribe every clip again; **Add a clip** adds another.

### Fine-tuned weights

(For voices from a clip and trained voices.) A voice can carry its own fine-tuned weights for an engine; today that is GPT-SoVITS: a GPT `.ckpt` and a SoVITS `.pth`.

1. Choose the **Engine**.
2. **Choose the gpt file (.ckpt)** and **Choose the sovits file (.pth)**.
3. Click **Add the weights**; when the upload is done it says **Weights for GPT-SoVITS added**.

The weights' version is read from the files. GPT-SoVITS models of the same version switch to these weights when they use the voice (marked **own weights** under **Which models can use it**); models of another version warn and clone as usual. **The voice keeps its clips too: the engine still needs a reference clip with the weights.** Weights exported by a training experiment are added to the voice for you.

### Which models can use it

Each installed model, and whether and how it can use the voice: **Clone**, **Clone (no transcript)**, **Preset speaker**, **Designed** or **Trained**, with badges such as **own weights**, **transcribed first** or **trimmed to fit**; models that cannot say why.

Rows that clone have a **Prepare** button: it computes and caches what the model needs from the clips ahead of time, so the first synthesis is faster. Otherwise the first synthesis does it once by itself.

### Try it

Choose a usable **Model**, change the **Text** if you like, and click **Speak**. The results are listed below.

### Details

**Name**, **Language**, **Tags** (comma-separated) and **Description**; click **Save** after changing them.

### The page footer

- **Delete**: moves the voice to the data folder's trash (`trash/`).
- **Export**: downloads a zip with the voice's details, clips and fine-tuned weights.
- **Use in Speak**: opens Speak with this voice.

## Import

Click **Import a voice** on the list and choose an exported zip.

## Command line

```bash
mizuoto voice add Me --ref me.wav                                     # from audio; missing transcripts by ASR
mizuoto voice design Narrator -m qwen3tts/1.7b-voice-design -d "A warm, low male voice, calm and slow"
mizuoto voice from-preset qwen3tts/1.7b-custom-voice vivian           # a preset speaker
mizuoto voice fit narrator -m qwen3tts/1.7b-base                      # whether and how a model can use it
mizuoto voice prepare narrator -m qwen3tts/1.7b-base                  # prepare ahead of time
mizuoto voice add-weights me -e gptsovits -f gpt=me.ckpt -f sovits=me.pth
mizuoto voice export narrator -o narrator.zip
mizuoto voice import narrator.zip
```

The other commands (`add-ref`, `set-transcript`, `retranscribe`, `edit`, `remove`…) are in `mizuoto voice --help` and the [command reference](/docs/cli-commands). `voice add`, `voice from-output` and `voice edit` take `--consent`, an optional note on whose voice it is that is stored with the voice (`voice info` prints it); nothing requires it.
