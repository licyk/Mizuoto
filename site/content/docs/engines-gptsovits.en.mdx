Few-shot cloning and voice fine-tuning ([RVC-Boss/GPT-SoVITS](https://github.com/RVC-Boss/GPT-SoVITS), MIT). Code: `mizuoto/engines/gptsovits`. On the CPU in fp32 with the same seed it is identical to Mizuoto 0.1 (upstream's `TTS_infer_pack.TTS` behind its adapter) in all 15 parity cases, and its trainer writes upstream's preparation files and weights identically. User-facing details: [Engines in depth](/docs/advanced-engines#gpt-sovits).

## Variants and files

| Variant | Catalogue model | Rate | Second stage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `v2` | `gptsovits/v2-base` | 32 kHz | SoVITS: a VITS decoder (coupling flow, HiFi-GAN) |
| `v2pro` | `gptsovits/v2pro-base` | 32 kHz | as v2, plus a speaker-verification embedding (ERes2NetV2) |
| `v2proplus` | `gptsovits/v2proplus-base` | 32 kHz | a larger v2Pro SoVITS |
| `v4` | `gptsovits/v4-base` | 48 kHz | `SynthesizerTrnV3`, a CFM with a DiT, a 48 kHz HiFi-GAN vocoder |
| `v5turbo` | `gptsovits/v5turbo-base` | 48 kHz | as v4 with `CFMV5` in four steps |

The variant comes from the base GPT and SoVITS pair in the model folder, laid out as upstream lays it out: `gsv-v2final-pretrained/…`, `s1v3.ckpt`, `v2Pro/s2Gv2Pro.pth` / `s2Gv2ProPlus.pth`, `gsv-v4-pretrained/{s2Gv4,vocoder}.pth`, `gsv-v5-pretrained/{s2Gv5turbo,vocoder}.pth`. A voice's own weights (`Voice.weights["gptsovits"]`: a GPT `.ckpt` and a SoVITS `.pth`, version read from the file) replace the base pair for that voice.

Shared assets: `gptsovits/encoders` (chinese-hubert-base, chinese-roberta-wwm-ext-large, the speaker-verification checkpoint), `gptsovits/g2pw`, `gptsovits/nltk-data`, `gptsovits/open-jtalk`; for training v2, v2Pro and v2ProPlus also the pretrained discriminators `gptsovits/s2d-v2`, `s2d-v2pro`, `s2d-v2proplus`.

Precision: fp16 on a CUDA GPU when `engines.gptsovits.dtype` is `auto` or `fp16`, fp32 otherwise.

## How it works

1. **Text front end**: language segmentation, then each language's grapheme-to-phoneme conversion into phone ids; Chinese also gets BERT features (chinese-roberta's third-last layer, repeated per phone).
2. **Reference**: the clip at 16 kHz with 0.3 s of silence appended; HuBERT's features quantised by SoVITS into the prompt's semantic tokens; the linear spectrogram for the style encoder; for v2Pro the speaker-verification embedding. v4/v5 also take the mel of the last clip read at 32 kHz as the flow's prompt.
3. **GPT (T2S)**: a 24-layer autoregressive transformer predicts 25 Hz semantic tokens from the prompt's phones and tokens plus the target's phones, batched with left padding, or row by row when streaming.
4. **SoVITS** (v2 family) decodes tokens, phones and the style into 32 kHz audio; v4/v5 make a mel with the flow, then the vocoder makes 48 kHz audio.
5. **Post-processing**: each fragment limited to a peak of 1, followed by the fragment pause, and the whole truncated to 16 bits.

## What reproduces upstream

- **Draws.** One generator, reseeded per segment, draws the T2S sampling and then the SoVITS (or flow) noise, as upstream's `set_seed` and the global stream did. Preparing the reference draws nothing (HuBERT's layer drop is not replayed; 0.1 prepared the reference before seeding for the same reason).
- **Pauses and output.** The pause after each fragment is counted at 32 kHz whatever the output rate, and the audio is truncated to 16 bits (`(x × 32768).astype(int16)`), as upstream returns it.
- **The text front end owns everything it uses**, built from asset paths: no `nltk.data.path`, fastText globals or `logging.basicConfig`.
  - jieba replaces jieba_fast (the same dictionary; identical on 20 446 texts);
  - OpenCC is the C++ `opencc==1.4.2` (the pure-Python port differs on 321 of 182 814 conversions, which feed G2PW);
  - G2PW runs in torch from its ONNX weights (identical pinyin);
  - NLTK's tagger and tokenizer, g2p_en, g2pk2, split-lang and fast-langdetect are ported, their data shipped;
  - Japanese needs `pyopenjtalk==0.4.1` (extra `mizuoto[gptsovits-ja]`): pyopenjtalk-plus changes OpenJTalk's rules, and 18 % of Japanese sentences came out differently with it. Korean needs MeCab-ko and jamo (extra `mizuoto[gptsovits-ko]`). The Japanese user dictionary is compiled once into the `gptsovits/open-jtalk` asset's `.mizuoto/` folder.
- **HuBERT's float16 checkpoint** is loaded into float32 parameters and its weight norm folded in float32, as transformers computes it (folded in float16 the features differed by 1e-4). On a GPU, where upstream calls `.half()` on the model and transformers computes the weight norm there at every call, it is folded on that device in float16, as it would be.
- **SoVITS's weight norm on a GPU**: upstream keeps it on the v2 and v4/v5 synthesizers (the flow's and the decoder's WaveNet layers, the WN encoder) and computes it at every call on the model's device in its dtype (`.half()`); Mizuoto folds it at load, on that device in that dtype (`fold_weight_norm(…, device=)`). Folded on the CPU in float32 the weights differed in about a third of their values, and the output with them (44 dB against 0.1 for v2). BERT is transformers' SDPA path, which transformers 4.57 picks for chinese-roberta.
- **Fine-tunes.** SoVITS checkpoints that pickle upstream's `utils.HParams` are read by a restricted unpickler (tensors, `OrderedDict`, `HParams` as a dict). v4/v5 LoRA files are merged without peft (W += B·A·α/r in float32, identical to peft's merge).

## Deviations

- **v4/v5's flow prompt** is always the last auxiliary clip; upstream's depends on the requests before.
- **Streaming speaks every text.** In streaming mode upstream speaks only the first text of each `batch_size` group.
- **Errors raise** instead of upstream's one second of 16 kHz silence.
- **Not ported**: upstream's CUDA-graph T2S (`t2s_accel`, which changes results), `set_seed`'s process-wide TF32 switch, and super-sampling (v3 only, not in the catalogue). On CPUs with bf16 matrix units upstream's `set_float32_matmul_precision("medium")` may use them; Mizuoto stays in fp32.

## Memory and speed

Phases: BERT around each text front-end call, HuBERT with SoVITS for the reference's semantic codes, the speaker-verification model (v2Pro), the GPT, then SoVITS (and the v4/v5 vocoder). The GPT is placed whole (its blocks read their fused projections directly; it is small, about 150 MB). A voice's own weights are switched in with `ModelHandle.replace`. The GPT's working memory is sized by the batch's longest row (its phones, the reference's codes and 16 codes a phone, within upstream's 1500 steps). With `compute.oom_fallback`, a batch that runs out of memory is spoken one text at a time.

The GPT is small and launch-bound: a step is about 360 small kernels and used to wait for the device twice (upstream's two end-of-sequence checks), plus a Python boolean copied to the device by top-p. Now the two checks are read together (the same boolean) and the copy is a `fill_`: one wait per step. With `compute.cuda_graphs` (NVIDIA, the GPT wholly on its device) its one-token steps run as graphs around each layer's attention, one set per number of rows (rows drop as they finish); the cache, grown by concatenation, and the sampling stay eager on the request's generator. A voice's own GPT gets its own graphs. The real v2, v2Pro and v4 models give the same audio with and without graphs, 1.3–2.0 times faster per request.

`compute.compile` wraps v4/v5's CFM estimator (each SoVITS loaded, a voice's own too): 7 % faster warm on the CPU, within 3.1e-5 of eager (87 dB).

## Training

Method `speaker` (stages `text`, `speech`, `semantic`, `sovits`, `gpt`, `export`): upstream's own recipe, without Lightning, torchmetrics, transformers, pandas or the ffmpeg program. The export is the voice's own pair of weights.

- **Upstream's files, identically.** Each stage writes upstream's files in upstream's layout, identical to those of its four preparation scripts: `inputs.list`, `2-name2text.txt`, `3-bert/`, `4-cnhubert/`, `5-wav32k/` (byte for byte), `7-sv_cn/`, `6-name2semantic.tsv`. Upstream's `load_audio` read clips through the ffmpeg program; `nn/dsp/swresample.py` is libswresample's default resampler, identical to ffmpeg 7.0.2 on every rate tried (soxr gave 43 dB).
- **GPT** (`train_s1.py`): the losses, accuracies, learning rates, the CPU generator's state at every step and the saved `<exp>-e<N>.ckpt` equal `s1_train.py`'s seeded alike (one and two epochs, batches of 1 and 4, DPO). Upstream draws while it builds the GPT (one encoder layer deep-copied 24 times, then the head) and once for the loader's base seed; `replay_construction_draws` repeats them. The bucket sampler's reseeding of the global `random` is a private `Random(epoch)`. `training_step` steps ScaledAdam at batch indices above 0 divisible by 4, so gradients pile up over five batches first and across an epoch's end; the schedule locks the rate to 0.002 after its first step, which runs at ScaledAdam's own 0.01. All kept.
- **SoVITS** (`train_s2.py`): `s2_train.py`'s and `s2_train_v3_lora.py`'s losses at every step and their saved weights tensor for tensor (half precision, version tag, no `enc_q`) on the CPU seeded alike, for v2, v2Pro, v2ProPlus, v4 and v5 Turbo (accumulation, gradient checkpointing, two epochs). v4 and v5 train SoVITS as a LoRA. Upstream lists clips from a `set`, whose order changes per process; they are sorted here. The WaveNet gate is TorchScript-compiled as upstream's (its gradients differ from eager ones in the last bits). Without the `s2d-*` asset the SoVITS stage refuses, as 0.1 did.
- Lightning's resumable checkpoint (930 MB) is not written. Compare runs with the same thread count: CPU kernels split their sums by it.
- `training.memory`: the speech stage frees HuBERT before the speaker model; the semantic stage puts only SoVITS's SSL projection and quantiser on the device.

## Parity

Identical sample for sample to 0.1 on the CPU through the stack in all 15 cases: cloning (Chinese, English), without a transcript, two references with a speed, batches cut at every punctuation mark, serial decoding, streamed v2 (token chunks, SOLA) and v4 (fragments), v4/v5's batched and serial flow, and a voice with its own v2Pro weights. At module level also the text front end (476 texts in 11 language modes), HuBERT, BERT, the speaker-verification model, LoRA merging and every decoder path. Four more cases load each implementation's trained voice in the other; identical too. The contract suite passes (24 tests).

On a GPU (an RTX 3090, fp16, 2026-10-19, stage by stage against 0.1): the codes, HuBERT, the text and style encoders are identical; v2 and v4 differ from 0.1 only by upstream's TorchScript WaveNet gate (`commons.fused_add_tanh_sigmoid_multiply`), which CUDA's fuser compiles after its first calls, so its bits depend on the shapes the process has already seen. With the gate scripted as upstream's (an experiment) both cases are identical sample for sample to 0.1; Mizuoto keeps the gate eager (v2 48.9 dB, v4 32.4 dB), for the reason VoxCPM's Snake runs eagerly ([VoxCPM](/docs/engines-voxcpm#deviations)): every request then gives the same bits. The T4 tier judges the two equivalent.

## Limitations

- Japanese and Korean text need their extras; without them such text fails with `capability_not_supported` and the install command in the hint. pyopenjtalk 0.4.1 is a source package that needs CMake and a C++ compiler.
- A streamed middle chunk too short for the SOLA overlap leaves an empty piece, on which upstream's post-processing raises an error; kept as upstream behaves. It has been seen with a `min_chunk_length` of 4.
