bilibili's zero-shot TTS ([index-tts/index-tts](https://github.com/index-tts/index-tts), bilibili Model Use License). The port is under the same licence, so it ships as a distribution of its own, `mizuoto-indextts` (`packages/native/indextts`, package `mizuoto_indextts`, with `LICENSE.txt`), registered under `mizuoto.engines` (`indextts = mizuoto_indextts:IndexTtsEngine`). Downloading and speaking need the licence accepted, and cloning needs a consent statement. On the CPU in fp32 with the same seed IndexTTS-1.5, 2 and 2.5 are identical to Mizuoto 0.1 through the whole stack. User-facing details: [Engines in depth](/docs/advanced-engines#indextts).

## Variants and files

| Variant | Catalogue model | Rate | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `v1_5` | `indextts/v1.5` | 24 kHz | Cloning without a transcript, Chinese and English |
| `v2` | `indextts/v2` | 22.05 kHz | Emotion from a clip, from eight weights, or from a description |
| `v2_5` | `indextts/v2.5` | 22.05 kHz | Japanese and Spanish, a language token, and a speed (`duration_factor = 1 / speed`) |

- 1.5 reads `config.yaml`, the GPT and BigVGAN checkpoints it names and the SentencePiece model; a voice's cache is `cond_mel.pt`.
- 2 and 2.5 read `config.yaml`, `gpt.pth`, `s2mel.pth`, `wav2vec2bert_stats.pt`, the speaker and emotion matrices, `glossary.yaml` and the emotion model's folder (`qwen0.6bemo4-merge`); 2.5 also `codec.pth` and its tiktoken vocabulary. Shared assets: `indextts/w2v-bert`, `indextts/campplus` (`campplus_cn_common.bin`), `indextts/bigvgan`, and for 2 only `indextts/semantic-codec` (2.5 never reads it, so its catalogue entry does not download it).

Precision: fp32 by default. On a CUDA GPU, IndexTTS-2 runs its GPT in fp16 when `engines.indextts.dtype` is `fp16`, and 2.5 in bf16 when it is `auto` or `bf16`, as 0.1 chose.

## How it works

Per text segment, IndexTTS-2:

1. **Voice prompt** (cached with the voice): the clip at 22.05 kHz (at most 15 s) and its 16 kHz copy; w2v-BERT 2.0's hidden state 17, normalised, conditions the GPT; the MaskGCT semantic codec quantises it into the decoder's prompt codes; CAM++ gives the style; the mel is the decoder's prompt.
2. **Emotion**: the speaker clip's, another clip's (mixed by `emo_alpha`), or eight weights mixed in through the emotion matrices (rows picked by CAM++ similarity); a description is turned into weights by the emotion model (Qwen3-0.6B, greedy).
3. **GPT** (`UnifiedVoice`, a 24-layer GPT-2): beam sampling of semantic codes, then a second pass over them for their latents.
4. **s2mel**: a length regulator and Seed-VC's DiT as a CFM (25 Euler steps, guidance 0.7) make the mel.
5. **BigVGAN v2** makes 22.05 kHz audio; segments are joined with silence, the tail faded and the result quantised to 16 bits, as upstream returns it.

2.5 conditions the GPT on the projected CAM++ embedding and a language embedding instead of the Conformer and perceiver over w2v-BERT, reads text through tiktoken with a `<|lang|>` prefix, and turns codes into decoder features with its own codec (RepCodec, a Vocos backbone, ×2 upsampling) instead of the GPT's latents. 1.5 has no emotion: the reference's log-mel conditions the GPT, and BigVGAN v1 with its ECAPA speaker encoder turns the GPT's latents directly into audio.

| Module | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `engine.py` | `IndexTTS2.infer` (2, 2.5) and the 1.5 path (`LoadedIndexTts15`): conditioning, emotion, segments, DiT, BigVGAN, fade-out |

## What reproduces upstream

- **GPT-2 as transformers 4.52 computes it**: `Conv1D` as `addmm`, tanh GELU, SDPA causal with no mask on the prompt and unmasked on each new token.
- **Beam sampling**, upstream's legacy transformers path: the log-softmax through repetition penalty (over every id, the prompt's placeholder ids included), temperature, top-k and top-p (keeping at least two), the beams' scores added, `2 × num_beams` candidates drawn without replacement, `BeamSearchScorer`'s hypotheses.
- **IndexTTS-1.5 without a KV cache** unless it runs in fp16, as upstream: each step reruns the whole sequence, and with a cache the sums would differ.
- **Draw order**: every draw comes from the request's generator in upstream's order (the GPT, then the DiT's noise).
- **On a GPU, where upstream computes what**: 1.5's reference mel on the CPU (upstream builds it there and moves it; a GPU's FFT rounds otherwise, and that alone changed which codes the beams settled on); the null position embeddings that transformers' GPT-2 adds in float32, so a cached step's fp16 or bf16 embeddings come out float32, as the prompt's are (`wpe_zeros`); the weight norms folded on the load device (upstream removes BigVGAN's after moving it to the GPU and computes the codec's and the CFM's there at every call; `norm_on`: the pairs moved there before `fold_weight_norm`).
- **The emotion embedding** is read from the clip again at 16 kHz, not from the 22.05 kHz copy resampled: it is a separate field of the voice cache.
- **Text (1.5, 2)** is normalised by WeTextProcessing's own FSTs applied with kaldifst. Upstream runs pynini on Linux and other FSTs elsewhere; Mizuoto gives the Linux behaviour on every platform. Names and pinyin are numbered in first-seen order (upstream uses a `set`'s order, which varies between runs).
- **2.5's text**: a language other than zh, en, ja or es (and an unset one) is sent as `zhen`, as upstream does. NeMo's Japanese and Spanish normalisation is absent upstream too, so that text passes unchanged; Japanese needs fugashi (`mizuoto-indextts[ja]`).

## Deviations

- **w2v-BERT's layer-drop draws are not made**: they never change the output in eval mode.
- **Sizes from the configuration.** Sizes upstream hard-codes (w2v-BERT's width, `gpt_layer`) come from the configuration, so tiny test models build; the real models give the same numbers.
- **Strict loading.** Checkpoints load strictly; upstream keeps random weights for missing keys (none are missing in the published checkpoints).
- **Not ported**: TensorRT, upstream's accelerated engine and the BigVGAN CUDA kernel (0.1's load options `use_accel`, `use_trt`, `trt_precision`, `use_cuda_kernel`), and 2.5's low-memory text split (upstream splits text into 40-character pieces on GPUs under 10 GB; Mizuoto's runtime manages memory instead). `use_torch_compile` turns on `compute.compile` for the s2mel DiT and BigVGAN.

## Memory and speed

Phases: w2v-BERT, CAM++, the semantic codec and the regulator for a voice that is not cached (or w2v-BERT for an emotion clip); the emotion model for an emotion text; the GPT (`kind="sequence"`; its GPT-2 `Conv1D` layers stream like linear layers); the s2mel; BigVGAN. A clone of a cached voice never places w2v-BERT or CAM++. If BigVGAN runs out of memory with `compute.oom_fallback` on, it decodes in tiles (IndexTTS 2/2.5). The GPT's working memory is sized by each segment: its text tokens and 24 codes a token (speech runs at about 5), within `max_mel_tokens`, for every beam, in the dtype it computes in.

Beam sampling used to wait for the device about ten times a step (`torch.multinomial`'s checks, the scorer reading each candidate with `.item()`, the stopping test, the beam reorder, the position embedding's index tensor). Now once: the draw is `multinomial`'s own `topk(p / Exp(1), 2 × beams)` (`runtime.sampling.multinomial_draws`, the same ids and generator state on the CPU and CUDA); the candidates' scores, tokens and beams are read back together and the scorer decides on the host from those values; the position embedding is its table's row. With `compute.cuda_graphs` (NVIDIA, the GPT wholly on its device, with a KV cache: IndexTTS 2/2.5, and 1.5 in fp16) the GPT-2's one-token steps of all beams run as graphs around each layer's attention; the cache, its beam reorder and the sampling stay eager. The same audio, 2.6–3.2 times faster per step on the 1.5's sizes. The beams' keys and values for every layer share one buffer (`BeamCaches`), which a step grows by a position with the beams' rows reordered in one copy per beam, each layer then writing its new key and value in place: each layer's keys and values are the contiguous tensors `index_select` and `torch.cat` made before, at half the copying (×1.11 on 30 s of speech).

`compute.compile` on a CPU in fp32: the s2mel DiT's warm time drops 7 %, BigVGAN's 20 %; the audio stays within 6.1e-5 of eager (87.7 dB).

## Training

None: IndexTTS publishes no training code for its TTS models, and the manifest declares no method.

## Parity

IndexTTS-1.5, 2 and 2.5 are identical sample for sample to 0.1 on the CPU in fp32 through the stack in all four cases: a 1.5 clone, a 2 clone, IndexTTS-2 with emotion weights and a 2.5 Chinese clone. At component level also the emotion model, every text front end, and the GPT with and without a KV cache. The contract suite passes (18 tests).

On a GPU (an RTX 3090, 2026-10-19, against 0.1 in each version's own precision): IndexTTS-2's two cases are identical; 1.5 (fp16) is within one int16 step (73 dB: its codes, GPT latents and BigVGAN output are identical, the step is the conversion), where it was 2.3 dB before the three fixes above; 2.5 (bf16) now has 0.1's length but not its samples (−2.6 dB): still open.

## Limitations

- No training method.
- 2.5's Japanese and Spanish text is not normalised (as upstream); Japanese needs the `ja` extra.
- Results may not be used to train other engines (`trains_others = false`), and cloning needs the consent statement the licence requires.
